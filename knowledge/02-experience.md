# Experience

Stefan has worked across agency, consulting, startup, and enterprise — fifteen years in digital product work. The progression: agencies first, then enterprise consulting, then a DHL startup as founding designer, then six years building DHL Group's central B2B portal. The current role brings the agency rigor, the startup speed, and the enterprise scale into one mandate.

Roles are listed most recent first.

---

## DHL Group (DHL Global Forwarding) — Head of Design

**April 2020 – present | Bonn, Germany (remote) | Reports to VP of Product**

Stefan leads experience design for myDHLi, DHL Global Forwarding's central B2B portal. The platform covers every customer touchpoint from quoting and booking to tracking and documentation across air, ocean, road, and rail. Today it serves 22,000+ enterprise customers across 50+ countries, generates 30M+ monthly interactions, and processes €336B+ in annual cargo value.

He reports to the VP of Product and owns product tracks alongside leading a distributed design team of up to eight across EMEA — product designers, UX, and researchers. Six years fully remote. The team, the operating model, the rituals, and the research function were all built from scratch. Dual-track agile, lightweight governance, shared ownership.

Research infrastructure didn't exist when Stefan arrived. He built it from zero — UserTesting.com, Hotjar, Adobe Analytics, qualitative loops wired into sprint cycles. He trained 50+ team members across product, development, and business units on UX fundamentals. The shift was tangible: the team stopped building what stakeholders requested and started building what data and users actually pointed to.

Scope continues to evolve — discovery tracks, design systems, conversational AI for customer support, automated quoting workflows, AI-augmented design ops via DHL's on-prem GenAI Hub. The common thread is making complex systems feel simple enough that customers trust them.

*Deeper case studies available: the US onboarding initiative that reduced setup from 10-12 days to under 24 hours; DHL Group's first scaled design system across multiple business units.*

## Saloodo! (DHL) — Lead UX Designer (Founding Designer)

**May 2018 – April 2020 | Cologne, Germany | Hybrid**

Saloodo! was DHL's bet on disrupting freight logistics — a digital marketplace connecting shippers with carriers. Stefan joined as the founding designer. No design system, no research function, no playbook. Small team, fast decisions, real consequences.

He owned design and product strategy as the marketplace scaled from a regional pilot to 50+ countries. The shipper base grew from 18,000 to 30,000 in six months. He shipped ML-powered carrier recommendations, dynamic pricing engines, and conversion funnel optimizations alongside the data and AI team — which is where he learned to design with model behavior rather than around it.

The MEA expansion was the chapter that taught him how research compounds. Dubai pilot to nine countries in six months, with Design Thinking workshops and user tests revealing that trust dynamics, payment norms, and communication channels needed fundamental UX adaptations, not translations. That became the blueprint for the global rollout.

The other artifact from this period: Stefan designed the initial concept for myDHLi at Saloodo!, before moving over to lead it full-time. The platform that became DHL Global Forwarding's flagship B2B portal started here.

*Deeper case study available: the MEA expansion playbook and the research-first methodology that powered it.*

## sunzinet AG — Senior UX Designer

**June 2016 – May 2018 | Cologne/Bonn, Germany**

sunzinet is a BVDW-certified digital agency with deep UX expertise through its long-term research partnership with Fraunhofer. As Senior UX Consultant, Stefan developed strategies and digital concepts for enterprise clients — BioNTech, KION Group, NKT, Yazaki, Berner Group, FrischeParadies, Sparkasse, and others.

The work was hands-on and client-facing. Stakeholder workshops, as-is / to-be analyses, concept documents running 200+ pages — from eCommerce platforms and omnichannel experiences to B2C portals and digital workplace solutions. Every project a new industry, a new business model, a new set of constraints.

The signature project was OBI Next, where Stefan led a 30-day sprint from workshop to MVP launch for a digital bathroom planner. He then embedded six months on-site to keep building. The methodology became OBI's blueprint for digital innovation — kitchen planner, garden planner, and eventually 14 configurators across 640+ stores in 10 countries all followed the same playbook.

Stefan worked on award-winning sunzinet projects during this period — including FrischeParadies and Berner Group, recognized at the German Design Award, and the Yazaki PowerYZer landing page, nominated in the same cycle. The agency was also a recurring winner of the German Brand Award.

*Deeper case study available: the OBI Next 30-day workshop-to-MVP sprint and its evolution into a multi-country, multi-configurator program.*
